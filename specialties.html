Ritual of Dining

The Traditional Telugu Meal Ritual

✦

Serving a traditional Telugu meal on a freshly cut green banana leaf (Arati Aaku) is an art steeped in ancient Ayurvedic wisdom and warm South Indian hospitality.

1. First Course (Aromatics & Ghee): Hot steamed rice, a dollop of pure melted desi ghee, yellow Mudda Pappu, fragrant Kandi Podi, and spicy Avakaya.
2. Second Course (Curries & Fries): Flavourful seasonal vegetable Vepudu (dry fry), stuffed Gutti Vankaya, or rich non-veg curries like Gongura Chicken.
3. Third Course (Digestives): Piping hot Sambar and pepper-infused Charu (Rasam) with crunchy Appadam, aiding digestion and awakening the senses.
4. Grand Finale (Cooling & Sweet): Creamy homemade curd (Perugu) with lime or mango pickle, followed by warm Bobbatlu or Double Ka Meetha.
