Welcome to Telugu Culinary Warmth

✦

Telugu culinary traditions represent a glorious tapestry of vibrant coastal Andhra spice, rich royal Deccan Nizami influences, and the hearty, grounded millet-rich dishes of Telangana. We bring these time-honoured culinary treasures alive with authentic recipes passed down through generations.

🌿

Gongura & Spices

Signature tangy sorrel leaves (Gongura), fiery Guntur red chillies, and sun-dried avakaya pickles creating unmistakable punchy flavours.

🍚

Pure Desi Ghee & Rice

Fragrant Sona Masoori and Basmati rice served piping hot with golden homemade ghee, comforting Mudda Pappu, and aromatic rasam.

🥘

Slow-Cooked Authenticity

Slow-simmered handis, clay-pot curries, and authentic Dum cooking techniques that lock in deep aroma and rich regional nuances.
